Cookie Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites.

How We Use Cookies

Fenton Forge uses minimal cookies:

  • Essential cookies: Required for site functionality (login, security)
  • Preference cookies: Remember your settings and choices
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand site usage (optional, privacy-preserving)

Third-Party Cookies

We do not use third-party tracking cookies. No Google Analytics. No Facebook pixels.

Managing Cookies

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Note that essential cookies are required for login functionality.
