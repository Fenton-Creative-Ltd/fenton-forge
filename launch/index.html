02 · HOW IT WORKS

From three words to an independent page.

  • IMAGINE

    Add three words

    Think “calm, friendly, modern” or “bold, creative, future”. The same words always produce the same starting style.

  • TRANSFORM

    Create your visual idea

    Your words guide the colour, layout and motion. Nothing is sent to an AI service.

  • CUSTOMISE

    Make it yours

    Edit the name, headline, description, services, contact link, colours and layout.

  • EXPORT

    Download HTML

    You get one independent file with your content and visual style built in.
