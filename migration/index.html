Frequently Asked Questions

Will my site go down during migration?

+

No. We build your new site on a preview URL. Only when you're 100% satisfied do we switch your DNS. The actual switchover takes seconds.

What happens to my old site?

+

We retain a complete backup for 30 days. If you ever need to revert, we can restore instantly. After 30 days, the backup is securely deleted.

Will my SEO be affected?

+

Positively. Faster load times improve rankings. We preserve all URLs, meta tags, and redirects. Most clients see ranking improvements within 2 weeks.

What about my domain and email?

+

You keep your domain—we just update where it points. Email is unaffected. We provide step-by-step DNS guidance or can handle the switchover for you.

Can I edit the site after migration?

+

Yes! You get full access to the Fenton Forge visual builder. Make changes anytime, just like any other site built on our platform.
