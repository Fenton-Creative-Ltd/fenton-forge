Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Our Promise: Your data never leaves UK jurisdiction. We don't sell your data. We don't use tracking cookies. We collect only what's necessary.

1. Who We Are

Fenton Forge is operated by Fenton Creative Ltd, a UK-registered company. We provide edge-native website building and hosting services.

2. What Data We Collect

  • Account data: Email, name, organisation (for account creation)
  • Site data: Content you upload to your website
  • Usage data: Page views, performance metrics (anonymised)
  • Payment data: Processed by Stripe (we don't store card details)

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

All data is stored on UK-based edge servers. We use Cloudflare's UK nodes and our own infrastructure. Your data never leaves UK jurisdiction.

4. Your Rights (GDPR)

  • Right to access your data
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to deletion ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing

5. Contact

Data Protection Officer: dpo@fenton-forge.com
